A. Multiple Finish Zones
- Added two finish zones to the level.
- The level has two different routes that give the player a choice:
1. A dangerous/shorter route with a vertical jumping section.
2. A longer route where the player must navigate around cactus hazards.

B. New Hazards
- Added a cactus hazard. If the player touches the cactus, the player respawns.
- Added a fall hazard. If the player misses the jumping platforms and falls, the player respawns.

C. Verticality
- Added an elevated jumping section to the dangerous/shortcut route.
- Added pillars as platforms that require the player to use the Spacebar to jump between them.
- If the player misses a jump, they fall and are respawned.

D. Theme / Sky
- Changed the level to a desert ruin theme.
- Changed the sky to fit the desert theme.
